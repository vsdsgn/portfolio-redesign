Case 03 / 04JetableMarketplace

Jetable: A marketplace is built table by table.

I co-founded Jetable, a hospitality booking marketplace: a guest app, a staff app and web booking, with booking live across six venues.

6Venues with booking live

01Situation

A marketplace needs both sides at once — and in hospitality, both sides meet in the room.

Guests want a table without friction; venues want a schedule they can run on a busy night. The product had to serve both across three surfaces, and bring its own demand in through the door.

02Role / scope

Role
Co-founder
Model
Hospitality booking marketplace
Surfaces
Guest app · Staff app · Web booking
Status
Booking live in 6 venues

03Decisions

  1. One reservation model, three surfaces

    Guest app, staff app and web booking all read and write the same booking, so what a guest sees and what staff see can’t drift apart.

  2. 15-minute slots, 90-minute cycle

    Time is a grid: bookable starts every 15 minutes, a table cycle of 90. Simple for guests to read, strict enough for staff to run.

  3. Acquire at the table and the door

    QR codes on tables and at the entrance turn people already in the venue into app users. Acquisition happens where the product is used.

  4. A marketplace, not a tool

    Venues bring supply, guests bring demand, and the platform holds the schedule between them.
