Kinopoisk Growth: Growth is a sequence of honest experiments.

Subscription growth at Kinopoisk: a sequence of experiments across the trial and onboarding path, measured on conversion, ARPU and retention.

+21%Onboarding purchase conversion

01Situation

Subscription growth is decided in a narrow window — between first open and first payment.

Trial and onboarding were where intent was won or lost. Every change there moved more than one number: a push for conversion could cost ARPU, and an easier entry could cost retention. The work needed a map of that window, and a way to test inside it without fooling ourselves.

02Role / scope

Role
Product design and growth — subscription experiments
Scope
Trial and onboarding path, purchase moments
Metrics
Conversion, ARPU, retention
Volume
11 experiments, wins and losses

03Decisions

  1. Draw the trial as a thermometer timeline

    Every step from first open to retention laid out on one line, with intent as temperature. Each experiment targets a specific point on that line, not “onboarding” in general.

  2. Read conversion, ARPU and retention together

    A variant only counts as a win if it doesn’t borrow from the other two. Three metrics, one verdict.

  3. Keep the losses on the record

    Across 11 experiments, the losing variants stayed documented next to the winners. A clear loss narrows the next hypothesis.

  4. Ship the winner into onboarding

    The winning onboarding variant shipped: purchase conversion from onboarding up 21%.
